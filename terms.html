Website terms.

Proposed terms for using this website and submitting a project-review request.

Developer note

This draft requires owner confirmation and review by qualified legal counsel before approval. It is not legal advice.

Informational purpose

This website describes Networks & Nodes, its capabilities, selected work, and current project-review process. Site content is general information and is not a project proposal or professional advice.

Project-review requests

Submitting the form requests a review of the information provided. It does not create a client relationship, reserve delivery capacity, or commit either party to a project.

Project terms

Any project scope, price, payment schedule, timeline, support arrangement, confidentiality obligation, ownership, and licensing terms must be defined in a separate written agreement accepted by both parties.

Your submissions

Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, regulated personal data, or other sensitive credentials through the project-review form. You are responsible for ensuring that you may share the information and links included in a request.

Case studies and website concepts

Pages identified as case studies describe documented project work and state when an outcome is client reported or still awaiting additional verification. Pages identified as a fictional concept, demonstration, or design direction are illustrative and do not represent an operating business, client engagement, available product, testimonial, or measured result.

External services and links

The site may link to third-party products, client websites, or service providers. Their availability, content, and terms are controlled by their respective operators.

Draft status

Draft updated August 7, 2026. Limitations of liability, warranty language, dispute terms, governing law, and the final effective date have intentionally not been supplied without owner and legal approval.
