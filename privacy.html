Site policy · Draft

Privacy notice.

How information submitted through this site is currently handled.

Developer note

This draft requires owner confirmation and review by qualified legal counsel before approval. It is not legal advice.

Information submitted through the project-review form

The form asks for your name, work email, company or organization, project details, and the type of service that may be relevant. Company information is optional; name, email, project details, and a service selection are required by the current form.

How form information is used

The current site sends project-review submissions to Networks & Nodes by email so the request can be reviewed and answered. The repository does not establish any additional marketing use or automated decision-making process.

Website analytics

The site uses Umami Cloud to measure a limited set of project-review interactions. The current implementation disables automatic pageview tracking and records only when the main project-review link is selected, form interaction begins, a submission succeeds, or a broad failure category occurs. Networks & Nodes does not add form names, email addresses, company names, project details, security tokens, or request identifiers to those analytics events. The script is also configured to honor browser Do Not Track settings and exclude URL search parameters and fragments.

Hosting, content delivery, and media

Vercel provides website hosting and the server function that receives project-review requests. Cloudflare provides domain, content-delivery, asset-storage, and security services. Mux provides streamed website video. Requests to these services may include ordinary technical information such as an IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, and request time under each provider’s applicable terms and privacy notice.

Spam protection and service providers

The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection. A submission token, request IP address, request identifier, action, and hostname are sent to Cloudflare for verification. Email delivery is handled through the site’s configured SMTP provider. The production SMTP provider still requires owner confirmation before this notice can be approved. These providers process information under their own terms and policies.

Retention and privacy requests

A formal retention period and process for access, correction, or deletion requests have not yet been approved. Until those policies are confirmed, send questions or requests to david@networksandnodes.org.

External links and website concepts

Project pages may link to client or product websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators, not by this draft notice. Forms shown inside pages labeled as fictional website concepts do not transmit or store their entries in the current implementation, although third-party fonts, images, video, and other assets used by a concept may still receive ordinary network requests.

Draft status

Draft updated August 7, 2026. The effective date and final policy language require owner and legal approval.
