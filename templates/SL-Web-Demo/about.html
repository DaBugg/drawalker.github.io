About S&L

Built on accountability.

A family-owned union contractor pairing generations of field knowledge with the coordination, controls, and communication complex work demands.

Our story

One van. A clear standard. Four decades of work.

Stephen Alonge founded S&L in the early 1980s with a straightforward goal: provide exceptional, cost-effective plumbing service and stand behind the work.

S&L grew from a basement office and one service van into a union commercial plumbing contractor trusted across public and private construction. Today, the next generation continues that work with the same focus on relationships, reliability, and disciplined delivery.

S&L’s published history states that the company has never failed to complete a contracted project since opening its doors.
