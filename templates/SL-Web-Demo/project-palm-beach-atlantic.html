Project profile

A vertical campus community designed for connection.

Palm Beach Atlantic University selected Gilbane to develop, design-build, and finance a new mixed-use residence hall intended to support campus growth, strengthen downtown presence, and create more connected student life.

According to Gilbane’s public project profile, the 25-story development at 1200 S. Dixie Highway will include 990 student beds in 275 units, with two-, three-, and four-bed configurations. Eleven student union spaces will support collaboration and community throughout the building.

The published plan also includes 28,000 square feet of dining space, an outdoor dining plaza with seating for 150, and 14,000 square feet of fitness space with modern equipment, group studios, and a golf simulator. An adjoining eight-and-a-half-story garage will provide parking for students and visitors.

Project facts and renderings are based on the public Gilbane project profile. S&L-specific trade scope is intentionally not stated pending approved documentation.

Published project team

Gilbane Development is identified as master developer, with Gilbane Building serving as design-builder and CUBE3 as lead architect. JP Morgan is listed as underwriter and Provident Group as conduit borrower. Completion is scheduled for Fall 2027.
