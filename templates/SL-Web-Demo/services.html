Services

Stronger planning. Cleaner execution.

S&L brings preconstruction, coordination, project controls, and skilled field installation together around the systems that keep a building working.

01 · Core capability

Commercial Plumbing

New construction and renovation plumbing for complex public and private facilities. S&L’s experience includes domestic water, sanitary and storm systems, equipment connections, specialty piping, and related testing and closeout.

  • Institutional and high-rise construction
  • Healthcare and medical facilities
  • Government, agency, and infrastructure work
  • Medical gas, acid waste, and process piping

02 · Coordination

Drafting + BIM

Our drafting team uses current 3D workflows to coordinate difficult installations before labor and material reach the field. The goal is practical: fewer clashes, clearer routing, and buildable details.

  • 3D model development and coordination
  • Multi-trade clash review
  • Shop drawings and installation planning
  • Field-informed constructability input

03 · Preconstruction

Estimating

Accurate estimating starts with knowing how the job will be built. S&L provides detailed takeoffs, scope review, and budget input grounded in decades of commercial plumbing experience.

  • Competitive bid estimating
  • Early budget pricing
  • Scope gap and risk review
  • Alternates and value analysis

04 · Delivery

Project Management

Project managers keep submittals, procurement, coordination, labor, and field decisions moving against the schedule. Potential issues are raised while there is still time to solve them efficiently.

  • Submittals and procurement tracking
  • Schedule and manpower planning
  • Coordination with construction managers and trades
  • Testing, documentation, and closeout
