# FleetAxis redesign notes

- **Archetype:** Freight-film landing page with an operations control-tower payoff
- **Audience:** Manufacturers, importers, contractors, distributors, and operations teams
- **Primary CTA:** Build a freight quote
- **Hero:** Mux freight footage with a high-contrast lane brief overlaid on the video
- **Playback:** Muted, looping, inline embed; Mux thumbnail remains underneath as a load/error poster and replaces motion when reduced motion is requested
- **Interaction:** Origin, destination, and equipment validation reveals the later control-tower planning timeline
- **Media:** Full-viewport freight video, port/facility handoff still, and active-jobsite still
- **Pacing:** Three chapters—service fit, control tower, and project cargo—followed by concise requirements, FAQ, and final CTA
- **Visual direction:** Deep navy, safety orange, electric blue, condensed industrial display type, and operational monospace labels
- **SEO/accessibility:** Noindex demo metadata, matching FAQ schema, labeled controls, inline errors, live form status, focus management, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion poster fallback
- **Legal:** Existing privacy, terms, 404, and fictional-concept disclosures remain in place
