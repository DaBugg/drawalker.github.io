Questions, answered

Credit guidance without vague promises.

Northstar provides monitoring, education, and consulting support. It is not a lender or credit bureau.

Does checking my credit here lower my score?

A monitoring view generally uses soft inquiry information, which does not lower a score. Confirm the exact data source and authorization during onboarding.

Can I monitor personal and business credit separately?

Yes. Northstar keeps the profiles distinct while making both easier to understand from one account.

What does a credit consultant actually do?

A consultant reviews reports, explains relevant factors, helps organize documentation, and creates prioritized educational next steps.

Can you guarantee a specific score increase?

No. Scores depend on many factors, and Northstar does not guarantee increases, approvals, deletions, or funding outcomes.

How long does credit improvement take?

Timelines vary with the starting profile, reporting cycles, documentation, and actions taken. The 90-day roadmap is a review structure, not a completion guarantee.

Do you dispute accurate information?

No. Accurate and verifiable information should not be disputed simply because it is unfavorable.

Is this a lender or credit bureau?

No. Northstar is a fictional monitoring, education, and consulting concept and is not affiliated with a bureau or lender.

What should I prepare for a credit review?

Bring your primary goal, relevant timeline, recent reports if available, and documentation connected to accounts or inquiries you want to discuss.
