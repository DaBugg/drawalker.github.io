Clinical boundaries

A mobility studio with physical therapy expertise.

Some clients need wellness focused stretching. Others need licensed physical therapy evaluation and treatment. The service recommendation depends on presentation, goals, and clinical appropriateness.

When referral may be needed

New trauma, unexplained neurological changes, severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, medical instability, or concerns outside the practice scope should be reviewed by an appropriate medical provider.
