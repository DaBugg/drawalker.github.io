# Meridian redesign notes

- **Archetype:** Editorial consulting statement and insight system
- **Reference:** Kearney's calm confidence, senior people, and thought leadership
- **Audience:** Owners, executives, leadership teams
- **Primary CTA:** Schedule a working session
- **Visual direction:** Soft white, ink, muted cobalt, narrow editorial columns and rules
- **Motion:** Minimal line and state transitions; no animation library, parallax, or scroll pinning
- **Libraries:** Native JS only
- **Interaction:** Four-stage clickable decision framework and expandable engagements
- **Media required:** Executive workshop, advisor portraits, decision map, research cover
- **Changed structure:** Decision problems, advisory areas, framework, representative engagements, people, thinking
- **SEO/accessibility:** Noindex, unique metadata, semantic details, labeled form, focus and reduced-motion support
