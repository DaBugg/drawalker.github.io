Before you disappear.

Can I join alone?

Yes. Most travelers join alone or with one friend. Groups stay small enough for people to know one another.

What does the price include?

Each field guide lists its exact inclusions. Most cover accommodation, local transportation, the trip lead, selected activities, and several group meals. Flights are usually separate.

How active are the routes?

Every route gives a plain-language pace note, typical walking distance, terrain, early starts, and optional activity details.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. A $149 deposit holds your place and the balance can be divided into scheduled payments before the final due date.
