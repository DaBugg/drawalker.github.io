RFI / 03

Questions to resolve before mobilization.

When should Structure House join a project?+

Ideally during site review or early design, when constructability, entitlement, insurance, budget, and long-lead procurement can still influence the plan.

Do you work from existing architectural plans?+

Yes. We can price and build from an established drawing set or coordinate architects and consultants through a design-build engagement.

How are schedule commitments managed?+

Each project receives a baseline schedule, milestone gates, decision deadlines, procurement tracking, and regular forecast updates.

Can you support investor reporting?+

Yes. Reporting can include installed work, milestone photographs, budget status, approved changes, procurement risks, and decisions required.

Which areas do you serve?+

Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade counties, with select work elsewhere in Florida.
