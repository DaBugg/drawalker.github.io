Technical FAQ

Before you send the package.

What should an RFQ include?

Include drawings, quantities, materials, tolerances, finish requirements, delivery location, and need date.

Can you provide material traceability?

Requested records can include material documents, inspection reports, and packing records.

Can releases follow installation sequence?

Yes. Packages can be planned by area, system, phase, or required-on-site date.
