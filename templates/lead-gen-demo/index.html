Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How much does it cost?
You pay a monthly fee plus your ad spend. If you close just one or two deals, you've already covered the cost.
I already use a CRM
No problem. We work on top of what you have. We send the good leads right to your current system.
What if it doesn't work?
15+ booked calls in 60 days or you don't pay us. Simple as that.
Will leads know it's automated?
No. Our messages sound like real people. Your team still does the actual calls and closes the deals.
I don't run ads
That's fine. This works with any way you get leads - website, referrals, Google, anything.
When will I see results?
Your leads get faster replies within 24 hours. Most people start seeing booked calls in the first 2 weeks.
