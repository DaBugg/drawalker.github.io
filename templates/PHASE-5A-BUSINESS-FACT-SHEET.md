## Verification standard

No production claim is considered verified because it appears in a concept page, draft, AI output, testimonial placeholder, or unsourced note.

Use these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | Supported by an approved first-party or authoritative source |
| Needs review | Supplied by the business but not yet approved or evidenced |
| Confidential | Verified internally but not approved for public use |
| Do not publish | Fictional, outdated, disputed, or unsupported |
| Not applicable | Does not apply to this business |

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Government, licensing, regulatory, or professional registry.
2. Signed company policy, contract language, or approved legal document.
3. Current company system of record, product documentation, or CRM.
4. Approved first-party website, profile, project record, or case study.
5. Named company owner or qualified subject-matter reviewer.
6. Third-party publication or platform with a clear original source.

Screenshots, links, PDFs, records, or named approvers should be retained internally for every material claim.

# Concept-selection rubric

Score each candidate from 0 to 3 against verified business facts.

- **0:** No match.
- **1:** Superficial visual or industry resemblance.
- **2:** Strong audience or offer match, with meaningful restructuring needed.
- **3:** Strong audience, buying process, primary action, proof, and content match.

| Criterion | Weight | Score |
|---|---:|---:|
| Industry and regulatory context | 3 | — |
| Primary audience/buyer | 3 | — |
| Primary offer and conversion | 3 | — |
| Buying or care journey | 3 | — |
| Proof and content types | 2 | — |
| Required interaction | 2 | — |
| Geographic/local-search model | 2 | — |
| Available media and systems | 1 | — |

Choose the concept with the highest evidence-supported score. Do not choose based on color, font, or hero preference alone.
