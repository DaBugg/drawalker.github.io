Case study 03 · Construction technology

S&L Plumbing and Heating Corp.

An ongoing engagement bringing a commercial website rebrand together with custom construction software for organizing submitted purchase-order information and locating received material across project floors.

Work in progress: The website rebrand and internal workflow are ongoing. Three-dimensional project views, LinkedIn outreach, and AI-assisted bid-opportunity discovery are planned or proposed rather than completed features.

Client focus
Plumbing · Heating · Construction operations
Current work
Website rebrand · Custom PO and material-location workflow
Future direction
3D project views · Outreach · Bid-opportunity automation

The custom workflow

Connect purchase-order information to where material lands.

  1. 01Submitted POsBring existing purchase-order information into the workflow
  2. 02Sorting mapsOrganize material information around the active project
  3. 03Floor locationsAssociate received material with its stored floor location
  4. 04Team lookupHelp authorized employees find where material is stored

Business problem

Connect digital records with where material moves in physical space.

S&L Plumbing and Heating Corp. manages project information alongside material that arrives and is stored on active job sites. The engagement addresses both the company’s public website and an internal workflow designed around how project teams receive and locate that material.

Website rebrand

Modernize the website around the company’s work and capabilities.

The website work is in progress. Its direction is to give general contractors, developers, and other decision-makers a clearer view of S&L’s capabilities and a more direct path to begin a conversation. The linked design preview shows the current direction; it is not the live client website.

Custom software

Translate a real material-receiving process into a practical tool.

Based on the project owner’s account, the internal workflow takes submitted PO information, applies sorting maps, and associates received material with floor locations. It is designed so authorized employees can use the shared project view to determine where material is stored.

Planned visualization

Extend floor-based lookup with a three-dimensional project view.

A 3D representation of the project is planned as a future phase. It has not been implemented, so the current case study describes only the submitted-PO, sorting-map, and floor-location workflow already in progress.

AI in construction

Explore AI-assisted bid discovery with human review.

A construction bid-opportunity finder is proposed or in progress as an expansion. The intended direction is to review bid sources, filter opportunities by criteria such as location and project type, and prepare useful matches for human review. LinkedIn outreach and advertising are also being considered for reaching relevant decision-makers. No AI bid-finding capability or campaign result is presented here as completed.

Current engagement status

A connected foundation for the company’s website and project operations.

The website rebrand and custom workflow remain ongoing. No traffic, lead, efficiency, revenue, or other quantitative outcome is claimed until it can be measured, verified, and approved for publication.
