Redeemed Hands

A WordPress service website that organizes a concrete, construction, and renovation offer around a clear quote path and a documented project gallery.

Client focus
Concrete · Construction · Renovations
Capabilities
Positioning · WordPress website · Quote path · Project gallery
Current status
Completed homepage preview; public launch URL needs owner verification

The service experience

Four elements that make the next step visible.

  1. 01Service storyClarify the construction offer
  2. 02Quote promptGive visitors a direct next action
  3. 03Project galleryShow an 18-item record of completed work
  4. 04Contact detailsMake the business reachable

Context

A local service business covering several kinds of project work.

The repository preview presents Redeemed Hands as an Easley, South Carolina business working across concrete, construction, and renovations.

Problem

A broad offer needed a clearer service story and quote path.

Visitors needed to understand the scope of the work, see evidence of completed projects, and know how to ask for a quote without working through an abstract company introduction.

Strategy + design

Put services, project evidence, and the inquiry action in one sequence.

The homepage structure introduces the service category, repeats the quote action where it matters, and uses the project gallery to support the offer with observable work.

Technology

Deliver the service experience as a WordPress website.

The stored homepage preview identifies WordPress hosting and documents the service content, quote prompts, 18-item gallery, and business contact details used in the delivered experience.

Current project status

Completed homepage preview documented in the repository.

A public launch URL and quantitative outcome have not been verified, so no external client-site link or measured result is published here.
