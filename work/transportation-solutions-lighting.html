Case study 01 · Enterprise systems

Transportation Solutions & Lighting

A connected operating system spanning the website, dispatch, reporting, and customer communication for a safety-infrastructure provider.

Client focus
Safety and transportation infrastructure
Capabilities
Website · Operations · Reporting · Communication
Reported outcome
~10 hours of manual dispatch work reduced per week

The connected system

Four touchpoints, one operating view.

  1. 01WebsiteClarify solutions and capture intent
  2. 02Customer communicationSet expectations and preserve context
  3. 03DispatchMove requests into a dependable workflow
  4. 04ReportingMake activity and next actions visible

Context

A complex offer serving complex environments.

Transportation Solutions & Lighting provides safety and infrastructure solutions across campuses, municipalities, roadways, intersections, construction sites, and other public environments. The digital experience needed to make specialized products understandable while supporting the operating work behind each request.

Problem

Separate touchpoints created avoidable manual work.

The website, customer communication, dispatch activity, and reporting needed to function as a connected journey rather than independent tools. Context had to move forward with each request so the team could act without rebuilding the same information by hand.

Strategy + design

Make the next action clear on both sides of the request.

The work centered on clearer solution pathways for customers and a more consistent handoff into operations. Each stage was given a defined job: explain the offer, capture useful intent, communicate what happens next, and keep the operating team informed.

Technology

Connect the experience to the operating system behind it.

Website, dispatch, reporting, and customer-communication workflows were treated as one system. That reduced duplicate handling and created a clearer path from the first request to the team responsible for delivering the work.

Reported project outcome

~10 hours of manual dispatch work reduced per week.

This is the existing reported result associated with the project. Additional operational measurements can be added when they are verified and approved for publication.
