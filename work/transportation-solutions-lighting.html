Case study 01 · Infrastructure + operations

Transportation Solutions & Lighting

A transportation infrastructure engagement spanning physical networking, ITS connectivity, server setup, cybersecurity, and the digital operations supporting customer communication, dispatch, and reporting.

Client focus
Safety and transportation infrastructure
Capabilities
Networks and ITS · Servers · Cybersecurity · Digital operations
Reported outcome
Reported manual dispatch work reduced through a connected operating workflow

The connected system

Infrastructure and operations, treated as one system.

  1. 01Physical networksBuild the foundation connecting the operating environment
  2. 02ITS connectivitySupport transportation-infrastructure systems and communication
  3. 03Servers + cybersecuritySet up the server environment and address system security
  4. 04Digital operationsConnect the website, communication, dispatch, and reporting

Context

A complex offer serving complex environments.

Transportation Solutions & Lighting provides safety and infrastructure solutions across campuses, municipalities, roadways, intersections, construction sites, and other public environments. The technical foundation and digital experience needed to support specialized infrastructure work as well as the operating journey behind each request.

Problem

Separate touchpoints created avoidable manual work.

Physical networks, ITS connectivity, servers, cybersecurity, the website, customer communication, dispatch activity, and reporting needed to function as a connected environment rather than independent layers. Context had to move forward with each request so the team could act without rebuilding the same information by hand.

Strategy + design

Make the next action clear on both sides of the request.

The work treated infrastructure, security, customer pathways, and operating handoffs as connected parts of one system. Each stage was given a defined job: support the environment, explain the offer, capture useful intent, communicate what happens next, and keep the operating team informed.

Technology

Connect the experience to the operating system behind it.

Physical networking and ITS connectivity were delivered alongside server setup and cybersecurity work, then connected to the website, dispatch, reporting, and customer-communication workflow. That joined the technical foundation to the operating path from the first request to the team responsible for delivery.

Reported project outcome

Reported manual dispatch work reduced through a connected operating workflow.

The project combined network and server infrastructure, cybersecurity, website requests, customer communication, dispatch, and reporting. The reported reduction applies to manual dispatch handling; additional infrastructure, security, or operational measurements will be published only when they are verified and approved.
