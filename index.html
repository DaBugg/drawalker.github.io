04 / Process

A calm path from friction to handoff.

Small, reviewable phases keep the system useful throughout the work and make ownership clear at launch.

  1. 01

    Map

    Find the customer, data, and operational handoffs that create friction.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    Choose the smallest connected build that changes the business outcome.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship in visible stages with performance, accessibility, and failure states included.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    Document decisions, ownership, measurement, and the next sensible improvement.

05 / About

The visible experience and the invisible system should agree.

I’m David Walker, the network and systems engineer behind Networks & Nodes. I help owner-led businesses connect customer-facing websites with the automation, analytics, and infrastructure that make the promise behind the page reliable.

A decade of designing networks, cloud systems, and internal tools taught me that naming, fallback plans, quiet logging, and clear ownership are what make visible work dependable. Those same habits shape mobile layouts, inquiry flows, dashboards, and integrations.

The goal is fewer moving parts, honest tradeoffs, and a system your team can understand after handoff. Creative and portfolio experience supports that work; business outcomes and operational clarity lead it.
